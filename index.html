About the College

Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College was established in 1956 under the Nankana Sahib Education Trust and is today one of Punjab’s oldest and best known engineering institutions. It is approved by AICTE, affiliated to I.K. Gujral Punjab Technical University, and was granted autonomous status by the UGC in 2012.

The college is accredited with an ‘A’ Grade by NAAC, is ISO 9001:2015 certified, and appeared in the NIRF 200–250 band in 2021. Around 30,000 graduates and 7,500 postgraduates from GNDEC are employed across the world.

Our Mission

  • Upliftment of rural students through technical education.
  • Respond to local societal needs by developing selected targeted research projects.
  • Quality training programmes in need-based modern technology.
  • Maintain state-of-the-art infrastructure in laboratories.
  • Promote a culture of self-employment.
  • Impart non-formal education to unemployed youth.
  • Inculcate moral, ethical and spiritual values in education at all levels.
